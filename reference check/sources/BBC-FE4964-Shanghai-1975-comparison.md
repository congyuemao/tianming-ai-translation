# FE/4964/BII/9—10：上海钟表厂工人参加管理（2026-10-16）

两份用户附件接成同一篇上海报道：`SHANGHAI_CITY_SERVICE__1975-07-25.pdf`为BII/9，`INNER_MONGOLIA_REGIONAL_SERVICE__1975-07-25.pdf`为BII/10，其顶部三行结束上海条目，再另起内蒙古农田建设报道。附件均原字节保存，见[第9页PDF](BBC-SWB-FE4964-BII9-1975-07-25.pdf)／[页图](BBC-SWB-FE4964-BII9-1975-07-25.png)、[第10页PDF](BBC-SWB-FE4964-BII10-1975-07-25.pdf)／[页图](BBC-SWB-FE4964-BII10-1975-07-25.png)。

BBC题名为 *Workers’ Management Groups Reinstated in a Shanghai Factory*。来源行为 **Shanghai city service 0700 gmt 18 July 75**，即1975年7月18日0700 GMT上海电台。报道对象写作 *Shanghai watch and clock parts factory*，所摘报道题为 *Cadres take part in labour, workers take part in management*。BBC明确标注“Précis, with quotations”，是带引文的摘要；两页覆盖该摘要的起止，不等于获得中文广播全文。

刊号与原书注98的4964/BII/9相应；刊日1975年7月25日据[Harris第146页注98](Harris-2015-p146.png)及两附件名。扫描页眉日期有残损，不声称25 July 1975在页眉上完整可读。

## 引句对照

[Harris第139页](Harris-source-p139-round44.png)引作：

> a number of workers have been *selected* to assume leading positions and take part in management work

BBC第9页首段对应部分（合并排版换行）：

> a number of workers have been selected to assume leading posts and take part in management work in various departments and offices by the factory.

Harris把BBC的leading posts写作leading positions，并止于management work；两者在所引部分含义相应。正文现译“一批工人被**挑选**出来，担任领导职务并参与管理工作”忠实表达该句，无需另改措辞。selected的着重是Harris所加，原注明确stress added，继续保留。只撤10-35的待核标记，不把回译称作中文广播原话。

BBC第9页后文另有：

> The factory’s Party committee selected a number of workers and asked them to take part in management work.

这对应Harris后文“the selection was made by the factory committee”。BBC具体写的是工厂党委，章末注据实注明，正文保留Harris的措辞与判断。

同段还记述当时的推荐及批准程序：

> Now the representatives of workers are recommended by the masses and they begin their management work after approval by the Party committee and after attending the study classes.

该句仅在来源对照中保留上下文，不把它加进作者正文，也不据此另添评价。

## 跨页续接

第9页最后一段止于“Workers are now invited to take”；第10页顶部从“part in study with the Party committee’s central study group”接起，到“genuine Marxists and workers”结束。合并换行并整理句读后的末段为：

> “Recently, the factory’s Party committee summed up the experience in having cadres take part in collective productive labour and having workers take part in management work and further developed the system. Workers are now invited to take part in study with the Party committee’s central study group and to discuss production tasks. Through various methods, the Party committee has striven to make sure that the factory’s leadership is in the hands of genuine Marxists and workers.”

下方新标题 *Farmland Improvement Conference in Inner Mongolia* 和其Huhhot广播来源行属于另一条报道；不应把文件名中的内蒙古当作上海报道的来源台。
