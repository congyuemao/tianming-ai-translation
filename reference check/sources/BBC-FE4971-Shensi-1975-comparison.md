完整核读题头下四段摘录。末段记述处理破坏革命生产、扰乱社会秩序者，为犯一般错误工人开学习班，严格考勤和岗位责任制。相关英文为（合并排版换行）：

> Having launched the masses to expose and criticize, they have dealt severely with bad people sabotaging the work of grasping revolution and promoting production and disturbing order in society. As to workers who have made ordinary mistakes, study classes have been held to help them to correct their errors. At the same time they have strictly observed work attendance and the post responsibility system and strengthened the workers’ sense of organization and discipline.
