# BBC记录与《天命》引文：第38轮逐条对照

本文件依据用户提供的扫描页面人工转录目标句，已逐页目视核对。不是自动OCR全文，也不是中文广播原稿。按用户明确批准的方式，以Harris引文为回译底本，BBC英文记录用于落实出处；本轮10处全部撤标，不再要求中文原稿或录音。

## 对照

### 10-39

BBC FE/4967/BII/4，第(i)则首段；Harris第142页／PDF161。

**Harris引文**

> unable to increase production under the pernicious influence of the counter-revolutionary revisionist line and bourgeois factionalism and due to the sabotage activities of a handful of class enemies

**BBC对应文字**

> During a certain period in the past, they were unable to increase production because of the pernicious influence of the counter-revolutionary revisionist line, bourgeois factionalism and sabotage by a handful of class enemies.

**回译／正文采用**

> 在反革命修正主义路线和资产阶级派性的恶劣影响下，又由于一小撮阶级敌人的破坏活动，无法增加产量

BBC段落的具体主语是杭州丝绸印染联合厂，Harris概括为杭州工人；按书中引文范围回译，不增入BBC的时间状语，也不据该引句独立证实军队接管的叙述。

### 10-40

BBC FE/4976/BII/6，七连段；Harris第142页／PDF161。

**Harris引文**

> they completed one day’s work in two hours and were praised by the workers

**BBC对应文字**

> They completed one day's work in two hours and were praised by the workers.

**回译／正文采用**

> 他们用两小时完成了一天的工作，受到工人赞扬

目标句一致。BBC上一句将具体工作记作(?taking inventory)，并在cement warehouse后标[as transmitted]；Harris只写worked at the cement warehouses，现译将额外增加的“装卸”改为“干活”。

### 10-41

BBC FE/4979/BII/5，第5段；Harris第142页／PDF161。

**Harris引文**

> They all took the initiative in carrying forward the same vigour...and worked continuously, fearing neither heat nor fatigue.

**BBC对应文字**

> they all took the initiative in carrying forward the same vigour, the same revolutionary enthusiasm and the same daring death-defying spirit displayed by their revolutionary predecessors in the revolutionary war years and worked continuously, fearing neither heat nor fatigue, in an effort to make greater contributions to industrial production.

**回译／正文采用**

> 他们都主动发扬同样的干劲……连续劳动，不怕炎热，也不怕疲劳

保留Harris的省略号及节引范围，不补入其省略的革命热情等长分句。

### 10-42

BBC FE/4979/BII/5，第6段；Harris第142页／PDF161。

**Harris引文**

> set a good example to the fighters by arduously working for a few hours

**BBC对应文字**

> While working at the Hangchow steam turbine works, (Li Chi-ming), commander of a unit, set a good example to the fighters by arduously working at a machine for a few hours.

**回译／正文采用**

> 苦干几个小时，为战士们树立了好榜样

BBC含at a machine，Harris未引，正文按Harris范围回译。BBC姓名为(Li Chi-ming)，Harris为Li Jiming；保留原书罗马字，不猜汉字，不以汉字姓名未定阻止引文落实。

### 10-43

BBC FE/4979/BII/5，第6段；Harris第142页／PDF161。

**Harris引文**

> actively took the lead in unloading coal. He never uttered a word despite the blisters on his hands

**BBC对应文字**

> He actively took the lead in unloading coal. He never uttered a word despite the blisters on his hands.

**回译／正文采用**

> 主动带头卸煤，尽管手上起了泡，也没有吭一声

两版目标句对应；BBC此前姓名拼作(Ni Chung-sheng)，原书为Ni Zhongsheng，照原书保留。将旧译“一句累也没喊”收回为“没有吭一声”，不加原句未说的累。

### 10-44

BBC FE/4979/BII/5，末段；Harris第142页／PDF161。

**Harris引文**

> In one day, ten cadres and fighters unloaded fifty tons of cement, and none of them complained of being tired.

**BBC对应文字**

> In one day, 10 cadres and fighters unloaded 50 tons of cement, and none of them complained of being tired.

**回译／正文采用**

> 十名干部战士一天卸下五十吨水泥，“没有一个人喊累”

十人、一天、五十吨及尾句均一致；按正文原有叙述与引语分界保留数字，将尾句忠实回译。

### 10-45

BBC FE/4967/BII/4，第(i)则第2段；Harris第142页／PDF161。

**Harris引文**

> vigorously denounce Liu Shaoqi and Lin Biao

**BBC对应文字**

> They have vigorously denounced the crimes of Liu Shao-chi and Lin Piao in trying to split the Party and the working class and repudiated bourgeois factionalism.

**回译／正文采用**

Harris作简短节引，正文按其范围保留，不用BBC较长句替换。现有译文可忠实表达该短句，仅撤标。

### 10-46

BBC FE/4967/BII/4，第(i)则第3段；Harris第142页／PDF161。

**Harris引文**

> cooks in the cafeteria strove to increase the variety of dishes. They provide the workers with steamed bread and hot vegetable soup.

**BBC对应文字**

> Cooks in the cafeterias strive to increase the variety of the dishes. They provide the workers with steamed bread and hot vegetable soup, and endeavour to satisfy the needs of the workers.

### 10-47

BBC FE/4967/BII/4，第(i)则第3段末；Harris第142—143页／PDF161—162。

**Harris引文**

> returned in the evening to drive the night shift

**BBC对应文字**

> After finishing their regular runs in the day time, bus drivers return to the factory in the evening to drive the night shift workers.

**回译／正文采用**

> 晚上又回来开夜班车

BBC具体指晚间回厂接送夜班工人；Harris简写为drive the night shift。依用户要求按书中引文回译，版本差异写入校注，不把中文译句称作原广播中文措辞。

### 10-48

BBC FE/4967/BII/4，第(i)则末段；Harris第143页／PDF162。

**Harris引文**

> have made use of their holidays and days off to do manual labour and have taken the lead in tackling the most difficult jobs

**BBC对应文字**

**回译／正文采用**
