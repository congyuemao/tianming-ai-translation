# FE/4973/BII/8—9：自留地与家庭副业引文（2026-10-16）

[第8页PDF](BBC-SWB-FE4973-BII8-1975-08-05.pdf)／[页图](BBC-SWB-FE4973-BII8-1975-08-05.png)来自KIRIN附件；[第9页PDF](BBC-SWB-FE4973-BII9-1975-08-05.pdf)／[页图](BBC-SWB-FE4973-BII9-1975-08-05.png)来自KANSU附件。刊日均为5 Aug 75。目标条目从第8页下部起，题为 *Kirin County Allows Small Farm Plots “at Present Stage”*，来源 **Changchun, Kirin provincial service 1130 gmt 28 July 75**，即长春吉林电台1975年7月28日1130 GMT。

第8页介绍Huaite县部分基层干部提议取消私人果树、自留地及农村市场，第9页上部接续该段，并记述县委书记(Hsu Wei-huan)组织学习政策。具体政策段为（合并换行）：

> This made them understand that, at the present stage, the commune members are allowed by policy to farm small plots for their personal needs and to engage in limited household sideline production as a supplementary part of the socialist economy. If all the small plots tilled for personal needs and household sideline production were abolished without considering the present levels of the force of production and of the consciousness of the people, this would dampen the masses’ enthusiasm for socialism and undermine the development of the forces of production.

[Harris第158页](Harris-source-p158-round44.png)实际引了其中两部分：

| Harris与BBC相应措辞 | 正文保留的回译 |
| --- | --- |
| a supplementary part of the socialist economy | 社会主义经济的补充部分 |
| would dampen the masses’ enthusiasm for socialism and undermine the development of the forces of production | 就会挫伤群众走社会主义道路的积极性，破坏生产力的发展 |

两处均相应。按照用户批准的BBC核对与忠实回译标准，11-17落实，撤一处待核标记。保留Harris所引范围及译稿原措辞，不将英文摘要额外文字补进作者正文；不称恢复中文广播原话。

[原注140](Harris-source-p163-round44.png)列BII/8，这是报道起页；目标句实际在BII/9，章末注36已补明。第9页上部是吉林续文，下方 *Lanchow Neighbourhood Courts Promote Proletarian Dictatorship* 才是甘肃另一报道。Hsu Wei-huan的汉字姓名未核定，不据音译臆造。哈希与范围见[本轮证据](round44-evidence.json)。
