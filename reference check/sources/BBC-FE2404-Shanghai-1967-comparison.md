# FE/2404：上海接管财产引文对照（2026-10-16）

## BBC版影与报道边界

[FE/2404/B/35](BBC-SWB-FE2404-B35.pdf)来自用户附件`SHENYANG_RADIO_BROADCAST__1967-02-28.pdf`。中部标题为 **Urgent Order by Shanghai Financial Supervision Group**，来源行是 **Shanghai regional service 23.10 GMT 27.2.67**，类型标注 **Precis of report**。以下是人工据版影转录的完整两段；仅合并排版换行，保留英文措辞：

> The Financial Supervision Group of the Liaison Headquarters For Opposing Economism of the Shanghai Revolutionary Committee, in co-ordination with other revolutionary organisations, today took charge of the property and funds of organisations of contract and temporary workers and liaison centres of young intellectuals working in rural areas, of youths working in support of the frontiers and of State farm workers.
>
> The Shanghai revolutionary organisations issued an urgent order demanding that all units stop supplying material and funds to the organisations of contract workers and temporary workers and the liaison centres.

该报道在本页结束，下一标题为 **Oath-taking Rally in Liaoning**。所获[B/36](BBC-SWB-FE2404-B36.pdf)上部续辽宁稿，中下部另起 **Nanchang Meeting of Cadres Addressed by Local PLA Commander**，来源行是 **Nanchang regional service 14.20 GMT 26.2.67**。两页均未见“十七个组织”拥护中央临时工通知的引句。此结论仅限所获两页，未查阅整期其余页面。

附件名均含1967-02-28，页内没有刊行日期；不把文件名日期作为已核刊日。上海广播的1967-02-27 2310 GMT已由来源行直接核定。

## 09-30：对应并落实

Harris[第126页](Harris-source-p126-round39.png)，仓库英文底本PDF145，原注64：

> today took over the property and funds of organizations of contract and temporary workers and liaison centres of young intellectuals working in rural areas, of youth working in support of frontiers, and of State farm workers

现有中文回译：

> 今天接管了合同工、临时工组织以及下乡知识青年、支边青年和国营农场工人联络站的财产和经费

两版财产、经费及组织类别对应，BBC的`took charge of`与Harris的`took over`均可译为“接管”。BBC写联络总站下的Financial Supervision Group协同其他革命组织接管，原书简写主语并省略协同说明。按原书节引范围保留引号内中文不变，撤除本处待核标记，在第8章注16补出报道标题、广播时间、摘要性质及版本差异。依用户已批准标准，视作BBC英文记录核对后的忠实回译，不称恢复中文广播原文。

## 09-31：继续待核

Harris紧接着引：

此句未见于所获上海摘要及B/36，不得凭邻近引注与09-30一起撤标。正文不变，继续查原注另列的 *Wen Hui Pao*, 28 February 1967，以及 *SCMP* 174, 10 April 1967, p.6（编号照原注，未独立核定）；若继续请BBC检索，应查另一篇“上海十七个组织响应中央临时工通知”的报道，目前没有确定的另一刊号或页码。
